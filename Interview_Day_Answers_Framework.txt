
INTERVIEW DAY ANSWERS FRAMEWORK (DEVOPS)
=======================================

STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE QUESTION
- Clarify environment (AWS, Kubernetes, Jenkins)

STEP 2: EXPLAIN YOUR APPROACH
- Monitoring
- Logs
- Root cause

STEP 3: ACTION TAKEN
- Fix / Rollback / Scale

STEP 4: PREVENTION
- Alerts, automation, best practices

COMMON PHRASES TO USE:
- "First I checked monitoring and logs"
- "Then I isolated the issue"
- "I fixed it and added prevention"

HR CONFIDENCE TIP:
- Speak slow
- Use real examples
- Don’t panic if you don’t know
